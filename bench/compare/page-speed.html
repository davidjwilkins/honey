Most WordPress sites load a Google Font, Google Analytics, a conversion pixel and something like Hotjar, and lead with a big featured image. This page does too, so that Lighthouse can say what Honey's page optimizations do for one.

Honey is a caching reverse proxy for WordPress. It keeps pages in memory, serves them straight away, refreshes them in the background when they change, and only sends WordPress the requests it has to.

With delayed scripts, the analytics and pixels wait until the visitor first interacts with the page. With the main image preloaded, the browser fetches the featured image first. With Google Fonts self-hosted, the font comes from the site itself.
